2. EXACT MECHANICS, FAIRNESS AND READABILITY [P]

2.1 Race model and clocks

Presentation-independent course graph: acyclic ordered legs with 4–10 legs for ordinary courses. Up to two two-way forks; each merges before the next fork/checkpoint. Tutorial exceptions are in section 4. Branches encode length, terrain/support, modifier intervals, cues and stable IDs. All entrants obey the same graph rules.

Fixed 20 ms simultaneous simulation ticks; record positions to 0.0001 abstract unit. Distance/time/rank never depends on rendering update order. Resolve same-tick finish by earliest within-tick crossing; an indistinguishable player/AI tie favors the player. Stable AI ID resolves their ties.

Three clocks are explicit: simulation active ms (actual racing ticks, used for pacing and meaningful-round minima), monotonic unpaused race stopwatch ms (records, includes dropped time due to ordinary slow frames), and permitted unpaused waiting ms (startup/ad recovery notices). None accrues during host/menu/recovery pause. A frame advances at most five ticks; discard excess simulation accumulation rather than render an unseen burst, but include the unpaused time in the record stopwatch so slowing the device cannot improve a record. A resource-loss recovery is an explicit pause owner, not an unrecorded slowdown. Performance validation must address persistent stalls.

2.2 Eight transports, three active slots and traversal

Three stable input slots: 1 Ground, 2 Water, 3 Air. The full included roster is Car, Dirt bike, Snowmobile, Train, Boat, Submarine, Helicopter and Plane. Core first-race set: Car / Boat / Helicopter. Ground variants replace Ground; Submarine replaces Boat; Plane replaces Helicopter. There are eight types across the game and exactly three controls in any race. Communicate the exact equipped names/capabilities before Play and keep that set fixed for the entire run, Replay, Restart and rematch. No loadout shop, eight-button toolbar or compulsory configuration step.

Six launch course families:
- Coastal: Car / Boat / Helicopter.
- Rough: Dirt bike / Boat / Helicopter.
- Rail: Train / Boat / Helicopter.
- Snow: Snowmobile / Boat / Helicopter.
- Deep water: Car / Submarine / Helicopter.
- Flight: Car / Boat / Plane.
Use one family per ordinary race. This gives all eight vehicles real course roles. Future combined specialty sets require complete legal connections/teaching/tests; this does not remove any named vehicle from the included roster.

Only supports allowed by the equipped family may occur. Snow requires Snow; Underwater requires Deep water; Rail requires Rail; Flight corridor requires Flight. Existing Air transfers require a Helicopter-equipped set and never occur in Flight. A course cannot demand an unprovided vehicle. Land/surface-water wrong-form recovery occurs only where this table gives positive speed and a comprehensible functional connection. Helicopter's positive supported-terrain speeds represent flight above it. Apply section-2.4 modifiers after this table.

At an incompatible entry, wait safely at the boundary while the race continues. A selection must have positive speed on the current support or the awaited entry support when stopped. Otherwise reject it with feedback, retain the current vehicle and queue no later surprise. Airborne Ground/Water commands retain Helicopter/Plane; submerged Ground/Air commands retain Submarine. At a supported exit, compatible changes are valid again. No floating cars, drowning, irreversible fall, repair charge or permanent stuck state. Every route must have an equipped legal continuation through its finish.

Train's role is fast connected Rail, with clearly cued junction routes and supported signal stops. Rail paths are continuous, branch and merge legally; no off-track drifting, derailment, manual steering or braking button. A signal uses the same section-2.4 gate cycle/maximum wait for every entrant; no hidden train-specific random delay. At a Rail end incompatible with Train, stop at the safe interchange until the player switches. Rail/Water or Rail/Air branches can trade length and signal timing. Do not call an always-worse route a meaningful choice. Cue a junction and needed change separately by >=800 ms at maximum legal speed.

Plane's role is a long straight Flight corridor between declared runway entry/exit boundaries on Land. Minimum corridor length 72 units. It takes off through the normal Air-slot command at a legal entry; automatic course-following handles the corridor. No extra pitch, lift, fuel or steering control. At corridor exit, land safely at its boundary; if the next support is incompatible, wait there for Ground/Water selection. Plane cannot hover over short helicopter transfers or fly under water. Flight-family courses use Land/Surface water/Flight corridor, with no helicopter-only gaps, Snow, Rail or Underwater. Wind later changes corridor speed under section 2.4. Plane therefore enables different course geometry, speed/read-ahead and landing rhythm from Helicopter, with no new in-race control.

Dirt bike is faster on rough Land, Snowmobile crosses Snow and Submarine crosses Underwater; Train and Plane add the actual rules above. The registry remains extensible beyond eight; adding more requires a useful decision/terrain distinction and the same acceptance coverage. A name/asset swap alone does not count as mechanical diversity.

A valid different-slot action applies next 20 ms tick with no cooldown/resource/speed tax. Same-slot taps do nothing. Latest valid action in one tick wins. Presentation reflects committed domain state promptly; no animation lock delays it. Host interruption cancels pending gestures, not committed actions. Keyboard 1/2/3 always means Ground/Water/Air and identifies the actual equipped vehicle.

2.3 One light mastery signal, no extra power system

Count clean transitions for feedback and replay goals. A supported terrain change is clean when the useful form is already active on entry, or a valid matching-form action occurs within 250 ms of first arrival and no longer obstruction occurred. An air launch uses first arrival at the launch boundary, not a later departure time after waiting. Each boundary awards at most one clean credit. Repeated taps cannot farm credits. A fork result is recorded separately; it is not a required perfect-tap test.

Display the per-run count and eligible total at the settled result. No speed boost, escalating multiplier, energy bar or progression lock is attached. Normal records improve through real route/switch choices. This supplies visible competence without adding an untested bonus economy. First two tutorial races emphasize understandable outcomes; the clean-change detail is optional to inspect, not an opening explanation wall.

2.4 Modifiers and route choices

Introduce each separately, and use at most one modifier at a location:
- Rough land, only on Land: Car multiplier 0.60, Dirt bike multiplier 1.20, Snowmobile multiplier 0.60; other forms unchanged. Teach that a flight-capable alternative can sometimes be faster locally.
- Water current: equipped Boat/Submarine multiplier 0.75 against / 1.25 with, only on Surface water/Underwater as legal; others unchanged. Underwater currents are introduced after the Submarine itself.
- Wind: equipped Helicopter/Plane multiplier 0.65 against / 1.20 with on their legal supports; others unchanged. Favorable Plane speed can reach 21.6 units/second, which must be included in all cue/input-distance validation.
- Gate/signal: on supported Land/Surface water/Rail only; 4,000 ms cycle, open 2,500 and closed 1,500, authored phase in 20 ms steps. Stop safely at its edge when closed. No crushing/pushing. Bypass routes are optional.

Order: support legality -> base speed -> fixed difficulty factor -> modifier -> gate -> boundary/finish. Spend remaining tick time under the next interval's rule, never tunnel through a closed gate. No entrant collisions, hidden boosts or random damage. Authored cue lookahead >=2,500 ms at the fastest reachable relevant speed, including favorable modifiers.

Forks offer two known destinations and a clearly indicated safe default. Swipe >=24 CSS px horizontally with horizontal displacement >=1.5 x vertical, or tap equivalent left/right choices. Last valid choice before lock wins; one gesture gives at most one choice. After lock, no backtracking. Both branches are passable. A poorer choice can lose time but cannot erase the run or require an ad.

Separate a fork decision and a required transform by >=800 ms travel at the maximum reachable speed; include wind/current effects when validating. At most one fork active. No exact timing is required to select the safe default. A route's relative cost comes from actual path, form and gate arrival phase. Do not label an always-slower branch a useful shortcut. Across authored route-choice pairs, either side must be the better option somewhere; demonstrate legal examples, not random route swapping after selection.

2.5 Rivals: real pressure without cheating

Exactly two local AI rivals. Explicitly describe them as computer opponents where identity matters. They use the same traversal/safety/gate rules and cannot teleport, inspect future inputs, arbitrarily stop to gift a win or change strength after an ad. Seed their decisions and save all relevant state. Profiles are fixed at race creation, never position-dependent.

Two named difficulties between races, using the same three in-race family-slot controls:
- Standard, default: tutorial foundation stages 1–3 factors A=0.78/B=0.86; foundation stages 4–6 A=0.86/B=0.92; later A=0.94/B=0.98. Terrain reactions A=220 ms/B=160 ms, with the explicit skill differences below.
- Expert, available after journey stage 6 completes: A=0.98/B=1.00. Reactions A=140 ms/B=100 ms. Expert records/medals are separate; it never becomes mandatory for Journey access, normal content or ad avoidance.

Seeded personality: A uses a 100 ms shorter reaction on Land/Snow/Rail but 100 ms longer on Surface water/Underwater; B does the reverse. Both retain their listed Air reaction for Air transfer/Flight corridor. Minimum reaction 20 ms. AI chooses the best equipped legal form using the same speed/modifier table, including specialized supports. At a fork, A estimates gate wait correctly but chooses the default on one preselected fork in every three fork-bearing races; B chooses the lowest current cost but makes one preselected extra 200 ms reaction delay at a terrain change per ordinary race. Preselection derives solely from course/run seed, not player position. Tutorials can omit these mistakes when no relevant decision exists. Expert A never takes a deliberately suboptimal fork; Expert B's extra delay is 100 ms. These modest fixed differences are proposals, not a validated optimal AI.

All entrants use the same equipped loadout and support rules. Every authored/generated course/profile and every checkpoint offered for rematch must have a legal first-place solution for a competent controller. Where that is impossible, reject/rebalance the course before offering it. Poor routes need not win, but every reachable route must be completable. A stronger course cannot simply require an upgrade. A perfect run can earn a large lead; never hide it with rubber-banding. Tune fixed profiles/course costs using novice/experienced traces, then freeze them per version.

3. FINISH, MEDALS, RESTART, REMATCH AND CONTINUATION [P]

Each run has a unique monotonic serial, mode, difficulty, course configuration/content version and seed. Replay creates a new run on the same configuration. Checkpoint rematch is an assisted continuation of the original run, not a new progression event.

Let the player finish even if both AI have already crossed. Their rank is settled, not a reason to terminate the player's payoff. Every genuine finish awards Gold=1st, Silver=2nd or Bronze=3rd. A finish of the current uncleared Journey stage commits its clear and advances the next-stage index exactly once. Finishing a historical Replay or assisted rematch cannot advance that index again. Gold is mastery, not a gate to ordinary new content. No purchased advantage, life limit, mandatory ad or currency is required. This is a proposed revision from V1's top-two-only unlock and early defeat.

A genuine finish requires crossing the authored/generated finish after actually starting and executing at least one valid different-slot action. A no-input traversal cannot silently award journey progression. Keep its result explanatory and Restart free. A meaningful finished round for proposed D ad pacing additionally requires >=15,000 simulation active ms. An unfinished Restart/abandon never counts. Best times require an unassisted genuine finish; rank and time are independent records.

Results remain until Next/Replay/Select/rematch. Show achieved rank, record improvement if any, clean changes, and the next ordinary course identity. Next is usable as soon as the settled outcome is present; no mandatory result dwell, spin wheel or extra collect screen. Next always advances the ordinary journey after a new clear; after a historical Replay it returns to the preserved journey frontier. Explicit Replay retains the exact course graph, loadout, generator/content version and seed so learning a correction is possible.

Restart is free through the race menu. Confirm only if discarding current-run work; preserve earned medals/records. It creates a new serial on the same configuration/seed, cannot reset pacing, does not reshuffle the future queue, and triggers no ad. Course selection preserves the existing run until the player explicitly starts a different one. Return to an acknowledged in-progress save offers Resume/Restart/Select while entrants stay still.

One or two checkpoints per course: author the first at a supported merged boundary between 25–40% of optimal course time; the second, if used, between 60–75%. Minimum 10 active seconds apart; do not create a checkpoint inside a fork/air transfer/flight corridor/underwater/gate block. Short tutorial courses may have only one or none and must declare it. Snapshot all entrants, reactions, gate phase, choices and run stopwatch at the same simulation tick, without recursively copying older checkpoints.

Optional rematch in proposal D: at a Silver/Bronze result, replay the final portion from the latest earned checkpoint with the entire race rewound consistently. The original medal/unlock is already earned and preserved. If the rematch is better, upgrade the medal, but record its time only in the assisted field. Never overwrite the original unassisted record or duplicate a unique-course completion. If it is worse, preserve the better original medal. AI remains unchanged; no opponent-freeze reward or paid guaranteed Gold. Free full Replay and ordinary Next stay available. Section 7 supplies exact ad/reward terms, including the separate optional next-set reward.

An assisted rematch finish does not add a second meaningful-round/ad-completion credit for the same run serial. Its newly played simulation time can advance the non-rewinding active-time cooldown, while every finish/unique-clear grant remains idempotent. Persist the original full-finish commitment when rewinding.

Finishing stage 36 settles its normal result and may say “Foundations complete”; Next continues directly to generated stage 37. There is no terminal campaign-complete screen, final numbered stage, finite recipe-unlock wall or required mode change. The ordinary numbered journey continues across launches. Generated stages are openly described as generated courses, not individually hand-authored new levels. Replay and exact Resume remain available. Bounded records and machine-counter safeguards must never end ordinary play.

A seed space is finite in a machine; do not promise mathematically infinite unique layouts. “Endless” means ongoing generated races and continuation without a designed content endpoint, not guaranteed novelty forever.

4.2 Procedural course grammar and exact limits

Each graph has a supported start, 4–10 ordered legs on each reachable start-to-finish path, a declared equipped loadout, zero to two separated two-way forks and zero to two supported gates; each fork merges before the next fork/checkpoint. The default route must contain >=2 consequential different-slot decisions, and every reachable route must permit a genuine action-taking finish; reject an all-one-form auto-finish layout. Use 0/1/2 forks with a proposed bag of 3/5/2 occurrences per ten generated stages, seeded and saved. Ordinary default path 360–600 units; target competent completion 30–60 active seconds including ordinary waits. Do not stretch racing to manufacture an ad gate. Foundation exceptions above are explicit. Snow, Underwater, Rail and Flight corridor intervals require the corresponding equipped set. A finish is supported, never inside an Air/Flight-corridor/Underwater transfer.

Generate a graph first, then realize terrain order, branch structure, lengths in 6-unit steps, gates/phases in 20 ms steps, legal modifiers, checkpoint boundaries and fixed AI seed. Parameters must create differences in useful switch rhythm, route cost, gate arrival or transport affordance. A new seed, changed color/name, small length jitter or reordered IDs alone is not a new meaningful structure. No 6-opening/6-middle/6-finale selection is the complete generator.

Non-neutral intervals normally >=24 units. Later rhythm intervals may be 18 only after stage 12, while still passing maximum-speed lookahead >=2500 ms and required fork/transform separation >=800 ms. At most one modifier per location. Gate wait never exceeds 1500 ms. Underwater must enter/exit through surface water with a usable cue and no conflicting fork/transform demand. Connectors declare their actual support and continuous legality; no unspecified fourth-terrain bridge. An AI and legal player controller must be able to finish every route; each offered course/profile and reward checkpoint must have a lawful Gold solution.

Randomness is local, deterministic and versioned. Input = generatorVersion, contentVersion, persistent world seed, stage index and candidate-attempt index. No network generation, downloaded courses, clock-dependent result or player-position/ad-dependent AI. Freeze exact generated graphs/loadouts/seeds for the current and next three stages. Reload, menu visits, ordinary ad outcomes and Replay do not reroll them. The sole explicit exception is consumption of an earned next-set choice under section 7.4, which replaces one unstarted generated course with the declared chosen-family graph; no hidden layout/difficulty improvement is attached. Restart is an exact retry. New generated configurations are reserved before their stage starts, not changed mid-race.

4.3 Repetition, pacing and difficulty

After the foundation, use a shuffled bag of all six taught transport families per six-stage block, with no immediate repeat at block boundaries when alternatives exist. Save bag/cursor. Vary 4–10-leg rhythms, two-form/three-form use, branch topology, modifier placement and consequential route trade-offs. Reject structural signatures in the last 12 stages when compatible alternatives exist; also reject a normalized layout identical to any of the last 64. Signatures include equipped set, support/decision sequence and meaningful route-cost pattern; phase/color/seed changes cannot evade the structure guard. No-fork races remain an intentional breather. Do not require all terrain types or all vehicles in every race.

Long-run variety has an explicit ceiling in difficulty: after stage 36, do not keep increasing AI speed, shortening cues or piling on more forks/vehicles. Rotate taught challenge families within the same safe bounds. Standard retains its fixed post-foundation AI profile; Expert remains optional. Alternate more demanding configurations with simpler ones through the fork bag and fixed complexity bounds. A very late level must remain physically playable with one finger and ordinary free vehicles.
