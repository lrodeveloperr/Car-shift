# Decisions

18 October 2026. User authorized implementing level generation, all feasible end-to-end tests and reporting the product-usable count. All eight vehicles and one-finger controls are in scope. Graphics remain unspecified. The diagnostic surface is a test fixture, not the final presentation. Monetization D is tested with fixtures only; no live SDK, advertisement or publication is authorized or claimed.

19 October 2026. User extended the roster: hovercraft, monster truck, drill, jet ski, glider and rocket, each with its own sounds, and updated levels. Each new vehicle owns a new terrain (marsh, wreck yard, mountain tunnel, rapids, canyon glide, rocket corridor), as the contract requires for registry extensions. The new families unlock at stages 37–67 and pass the same audit, simulation, end-to-end and stress gates as the original six. The original six families and the 36 teaching stages regenerate byte-identically, so existing saves stay valid; older six-family saves migrate their course cursors. User direction on ads, recorded and not implemented: rely on YouTube's automatic pre-roll, and make retrying a race always a rewarded video. This conflicts with the current contract's free retry. It also needs a free fallback when no ad is available, because YouTube serves ads only to some players.

19 October 2026, later. User asked for a single-screen game with no level picking, and approved keeping the prevalidated bank as the core plus a daily course. The daily is generated on the device from the date, has no menu, and simply starts from Play as the first race of each day. It must pass the same gates as the bank, never advances the journey, and keeps a streak. The bank stays at 9,577 courses: keeping every valid layout (114,281 courses, 48,412 distinct structures) would cost about 1.9 MB compressed and several seconds of startup on phones for content few players would reach. Later the same day, the user removed all daily-specific visuals and sounds. The flow is only Play, then Next; the daily is indistinguishable from any other level.

18 November 2026. Mediacube approved the game for publication, and the user approved this ad plan. Pre-roll is YouTube's. Interstitials run on Next only: none in stages 1–5, none right before a vehicle debut, at most one every 2 races and 2 minutes, and none within 2 minutes of a rewarded video. There is one rewarded offer per result, and Next stays free. A loss offers a retry: from the checkpoint when gold is reachable, else from the start. A win alternates by stage between a one-race trial of the next locked family (not an unlock, so the contract's no-video-unlock rule holds) and a new paint. These change the contract in three ways. Retry now goes through a rewarded video, but is free when no ad is available. Rewarded offers no longer wait out a 2-minute cooldown. Interstitials start at stage 6.

18 November 2026, later. User restructured play around levels and asked for more competitive opponents and maximum vehicle variety. Changes:
- Only first place clears a level; retry is free; a rewarded video skips a lost level. Interstitials come only after wins.
- After a win, the vehicle-trial and paint offers stay.
- A level number sits top left; there is no pause button, because YouTube pauses the game.
- Standard opponents after level 6 are 0.952/0.982 speed with 205/152 ms reactions. On a third of the bank, 150 ms simulated switches win 99%, 300 ms win 64%, and slow switches with a mistake win 1%. Perfect play still wins every course.
- A family unlocks every three levels, so all 14 vehicles arrive by level 33. Teaching levels rotate to the least recently raced family.
- The daily course became the day's first level after the teaching levels, so the level number never repeats after a win.

Prebuilt content is counted after mathematical/simulation/pacing/diversity acceptance. Human enjoyment is not established by synthetic players. The earlier 5,580 count is a restricted sequence enumeration, not a certified level count. Keep rejection evidence. Do not relax quality gates just to meet a number.
