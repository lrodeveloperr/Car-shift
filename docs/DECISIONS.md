# Decisions

18 October 2026. User authorized implementing level generation, all feasible end-to-end tests and reporting the product-usable count. All eight vehicles and one-finger controls are in scope. Graphics remain unspecified. The diagnostic surface is a test fixture, not the final presentation. Monetization D is tested with fixtures only; no live SDK, advertisement or publication is authorized or claimed.

19 October 2026. User extended the roster: hovercraft, monster truck, drill, jet ski, glider and rocket, each with its own sounds, and updated levels. Each new vehicle owns a new terrain (marsh, wreck yard, mountain tunnel, rapids, canyon glide, rocket corridor), as the contract requires for registry extensions. The new families unlock at stages 37–67 and pass the same audit, simulation, end-to-end and stress gates as the original six. The original six families and the 36 teaching stages regenerate byte-identically, so existing saves stay valid; older six-family saves migrate their course cursors. User direction on ads, recorded and not implemented: rely on YouTube's automatic pre-roll, and make retrying a race always a rewarded video. This conflicts with the current contract's free retry. It also needs a free fallback when no ad is available, because YouTube serves ads only to some players.

19 October 2026, later. User asked for a single-screen game with no level picking, and approved keeping the prevalidated bank as the core plus a daily course. The daily is generated on the device from the date, has no menu, and simply starts from Play as the first race of each day. It must pass the same gates as the bank, never advances the journey, and keeps a streak. The bank stays at 9,577 courses: keeping every valid layout (114,281 courses, 48,412 distinct structures) would cost about 1.9 MB compressed and several seconds of startup on phones for content few players would reach.

Prebuilt content is counted after mathematical/simulation/pacing/diversity acceptance. Human enjoyment is not established by synthetic players. The earlier 5,580 count is a restricted sequence enumeration, not a certified level count. Keep rejection evidence. Do not relax quality gates just to meet a number.
