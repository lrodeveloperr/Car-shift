## What “enjoyable” means here

The checks reject mechanical failure modes: repeated 2/3-symbol motifs, long decision gaps, one form dominating travel, unsupported specialty connections, invalid checkpoint placement, unusable branches, unreachable routes and impossible Gold. They establish plausible, coherent race content under the declared rules.

**No automated test establishes enjoyment, engagement, comparative superiority or monetization.** Final presentation and real play could still reveal confusing specialty slots, repetitive rhythms, weak perceived competition or unsatisfying feedback. The planned adult novice/comparator preference gate in the contract has NOT RUN. No acquisition, retention, served-ad, eCPM or developer-net revenue result exists for this candidate.

## Remaining acceptance

Native browser engines could not be installed: the official download returned an unavailable HTML page. Browser security rejected opening the local QA file. No alternate browser route was attempted after that rejection. Consequently native Chromium/Firefox/WebKit/Edge, CSS/viewport/layout, physical Android/iOS, genuine YouTube SDK/cloud/ads/mute and host certification remain BLOCKED or NOT RUN. Graphics remain unspecified; the semantic diagnostic is not the game's final presentation. Ten production locales/accessibility/comparator tests are not certified.

All 75 contract cases are assessed: 20 PASS in their stated standalone sub-scope, 51 NOT RUN for their complete required method, and 4 inapplicable to this diagnostic revision. A standalone PASS is not production acceptance. The local-framework release check is expected to retain these blockers. There is no publish/promote action.

Proposal D remains disabled by default and fixture-only. It tests first/later 2 finishes AND 120 active-second guards and optional rematch/next-family credits. There are no real SDK or ad calls, no account/backend, no personal data or external analytics. A crash between an unreceipted host completion and durable reward acknowledgement can lose that reward; host receipt/reconciliation cannot be promised by these fixtures.
